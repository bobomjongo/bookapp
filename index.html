Chapter One
The Road North

The harmattan came early that year, coating everything in Kano with a fine red dust that tasted of the Sahara. Amara pressed her face against the bus window and watched the minarets recede behind a gauze of heat and particulate, already uncertain whether she was leaving or being expelled.

She had not told anyone she was going. Not her mother, who would have wept. Not Dele, who would have laughed. Certainly not the man in the grey kaftan who had handed her the envelope three weeks ago on Ahmadu Bello Way, as though passing salt across a table.
